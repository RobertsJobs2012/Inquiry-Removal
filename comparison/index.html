When a Competitor Might Actually Be the Better Choice — We'll Tell You Honestly

This section exists because we believe in giving you complete information, not just the parts that make us look good.

Inquiry Removal is not the right fit if

You need more than inquiry removal. If your credit report has collections, charge-offs, late payments, bankruptcies, or other negative items beyond hard inquiries — Inquiry Removal is not the company for you. We remove hard inquiries exclusively. We are genuinely the best in the country at that one thing, but it is the only thing we do. If your situation requires a broader credit repair strategy, a company like Credit Saint or Lexington Law covers a wider range of negative items. The monthly fee is real and the results are slower, but the scope is broader — and if your needs extend beyond inquiries, that scope matters. You have no unauthorized or excessive hard inquiries. Not every hard inquiry qualifies for removal. Inquiries from credit applications you genuinely authorized — and that were not excessive or improperly obtained — have a much weaker legal argument for dispute. If your free consultation reveals that your inquiries are all legitimately authorized and properly documented, we will tell you that clearly rather than take your money on a case we cannot win. You want an in-person office relationship. We operate entirely online. There is no physical location, no office to visit, and no in-person appointments. If that matters to you, a company with a local presence may be a better fit.

Honest acknowledgment: We know that saying "here's when you shouldn't hire us" is not standard marketing practice. We do it because the clients who trust us most are the ones who trusted us enough to hear the full picture first — not a curated version of it.
