Section 2: Your Consumer Rights Under Federal Law

Render as H2: Your Consumer Rights Under Federal Law

NOTICE REQUIRED BY FEDERAL LAW

As required by the Credit Repair Organizations Act (15 U.S.C. § 1679c), Inquiry Removal is providing the following statement of your rights before any contract for services is entered into:

Consumer Credit File Rights Under State and Federal Law

You have a right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report by contacting the credit bureau directly. There is no fee for this. Any legitimate credit repair organization is prohibited from taking money from you before completing any promised services. You can cancel your contract with any credit repair organization for any reason within 3 business days from the date you signed it. Credit repair organizations are required to provide you with a statement describing your legal rights before you sign a contract. You may also contact the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) at www.consumerfinance.gov or 1-855-411-2372 and the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) at www.ftc.gov or 1-877-382-4357 for more information about your rights.

Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq.), you have the right to:

  • Obtain a free copy of your credit report from each of the three major credit bureaus once every 12 months at AnnualCreditReport.com
  • Dispute inaccurate or incomplete information in your credit report directly with the credit bureau at no charge
  • Have inaccurate information corrected or deleted
  • Seek damages from anyone who violates your rights under the FCRA

Under the Credit Repair Organizations Act (15 U.S.C. § 1679 et seq.), you have the right to:

  • Receive a complete and accurate disclosure of the services to be performed and their cost before signing any contract
  • Cancel any signed contract within 3 business days of signing without penalty or obligation
  • Sue a credit repair organization that violates the CROA in federal court for actual damages, punitive damages, and attorney fees
  • Report violations to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and the Federal Trade Commission

Under Arizona law (A.R.S. § 44-1701 et seq.), you have additional rights as a consumer dealing with a credit services organization operating in the state of Arizona.

Section 3: The Services We Provide

Render as H2: The Services We Provide

Inquiry Removal provides hard inquiry dispute assistance services. Specifically, we:

  • Review consumer credit reports across Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion to identify hard inquiries that may be unauthorized, inaccurate, excessive, or fraudulent
  • Prepare custom dispute correspondence on behalf of the client, tailored to the specific inquiry type and applicable federal law, including the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), the Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act (FACTA), and Metro 2 compliance standards
  • Submit disputes to the relevant credit reporting agencies on behalf of the client
  • Manage all follow-up communications with credit reporting agencies throughout the dispute process
  • Provide the client with a post-deletion credit protection strategy upon completion of services

What we do not do. Inquiry Removal does not provide services related to collections, late payments, charge-offs, bankruptcies, judgments, or any negative credit item other than hard inquiries. We do not dispute legitimate, authorized inquiries that the client agreed to. We do not guarantee any particular credit score improvement — individual results vary based on each client's credit profile, inquiry type, bureau response, and other factors outside our control.

No legal advice. Nothing in these Terms of Service, on this website, or in any communication from Inquiry Removal constitutes legal advice. We are not a law firm and Robert Garcia is not your attorney. If you need legal advice regarding your rights under the FCRA or any other law, you should consult a licensed attorney.
