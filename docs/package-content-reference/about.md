The credit repair industry has a serious trust problem. And it earned it.
Most credit repair companies — including some of the biggest names you've seen advertised — operate on a monthly subscription model. That structure creates a quiet but powerful conflict of interest: the longer your credit stays broken, the more money they make. There is no financial incentive for a subscription-based company to work fast.
